# Triage policy (Shelf)

- Product direction and scope live in `MISSION.md`. Anything on its out-of-scope list is `NO_ACTION` with a note, never planned.
- A user-visible change to the page (layout, copy, a new control) with no open product question is implementation-ready: route `deliver`.
- A request that leaves a product choice open (what the control does, which options exist, defaults) routes `plan`.
